# Guest Guide: ordering with your phone

No app and no account are needed. This guide is also in the app at **/help/guests**, and restaurants can print it for their tables.

## At the table

1. **Scan** the QR code on your table with your phone camera and open the link.
2. **Enter your name.** A phone number is optional. Add it only if you want the restaurant to recognise you next time.
3. **Browse** the menu by category. Tap an item to choose a size, extras and notes, then add it.
4. Open **Your order**, check it, and tap **Send order**.
5. **Track** the status: *Waiting for confirmation*, then *Accepted*, *Preparing*, *Ready* and *Served*, with an estimated time.

### Changing your order

- You can edit a just-sent order for a few minutes. The restaurant sets how long.
- After that, tap **Add more items**. They go onto the same bill.

### Closed the browser or the phone died?

Scan the same QR again. Your order and bill come back automatically. Friends at your table can join by entering the **4-digit table PIN** shown on your screen.

## Help is one tap away

Tap the help button in the bottom-right corner for:
- **Call waiter**
- **Billing help**
- **Chat**: type a message to the staff.
- **Voice call**: talk to staff through the browser. It asks for microphone permission. If the call cannot connect, you are offered the restaurant's phone number or a call-back request.

**Wi-Fi:** if the restaurant displays a Wi-Fi QR code, scan it with your camera to join without typing the password.

## Paying

1. Open **Bill**. You see every item, discount and each tax separately.
2. Choose how to pay:
   - **Bank or wallet transfer**: pay in your banking app, then upload the screenshot. The bill shows *Under review* until staff confirm it.
   - **Pay cash**: a staff member comes to collect.
   - Card or cash at the counter is always possible.
3. After you are served, you can leave a rating and feedback.

## Ordering online (pickup or delivery)

1. Open the restaurant's website (`/r/<restaurant>`), or scan its online-ordering QR from a flyer or social media.
2. Choose **Pickup** or **Delivery**. Delivery may have a charge or a minimum order.
3. Order and pay the same way as at a table, then track your order live.
4. You can **book a table** from the website too.

## Your privacy

- Only your name is required.
- Being recognised next time and receiving offers are **off unless you tick them**.
- Your session is kept on your device with a secure cookie so you can come back to your order. It expires automatically.
- To have your data deleted, ask the restaurant. They can anonymize your profile.
- See the full **Privacy Policy** at `/privacy` and the **Terms** at `/terms`.
