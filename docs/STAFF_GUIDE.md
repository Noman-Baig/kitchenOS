# Staff Guide (waiters, kitchen, cashiers)

Sign in at `/login` with the email and password your manager created. You only see the screens your role allows. The **Live** badge at the top means real-time updates are connected. If it shows **Reconnecting…**, keep working: the screen resyncs by itself when the connection returns. Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, or ask your manager.

## Waiters

Open **Waiter** on your phone. Add it to your home screen to get an app-like icon.

| Section | What to do |
|---|---|
| **My tables** | Live state of your tables: free, ordering, waiting, eating, bill requested |
| **Ready** | Orders the kitchen has finished. Deliver them and tap **Mark served**. |
| **Requests** | Waiter call, billing help, cash collection, call-back. Tap **Acknowledge** when you go and **Resolve** when done. |
| **Tasks** | Tasks from your manager. Accept, start, complete. Use **Report issue** for broken equipment. |
| **New order** | For guests without a smartphone: choose the table, enter the guest's name, add items, variants and notes, then **Send**. It goes straight to the kitchen. To add more items later, open the order and choose **Add items**. |

When a guest starts a voice call, your phone rings in the app if your role receives calls. Tap **Answer**. Your browser asks for microphone permission the first time.

## Kitchen

1. Open **Kitchen display** on a tablet or screen and select your station(s).
2. New tickets appear with a sound. You can toggle the sound in the header.
3. Tap **Start** to begin. The guest then sees *Preparing*.
4. Tap **Ready** when your station's items are done. When every station is ready, the order becomes **Ready** and waiters are notified.
5. Timers count from arrival. A ticket turns **red** when it passes its estimate or the delay threshold. **Priority** tickets have an orange edge.
6. If an item runs out, ask a manager to switch it off in **Menu items**. It disappears from guest menus instantly.
7. Record dropped, expired or damaged stock under **Wastage & comps**. A manager approves it.

## Cashiers (POS)

1. **Open shift**: enter the opening cash in the drawer.
2. **Sell**:
   1. Search or tap items, then choose variants and add-ons.
   2. Pick the order type: dine-in (with table), takeaway or pickup.
   3. Apply a discount or coupon (if your role allows it) and select optional taxes.
   4. Tap **Charge**.
   5. Add one or more payments: cash, card, bank or wallet. For cash, enter the amount tendered to see the change.
3. The bill closes automatically when fully paid. Print the receipt (browser, USB, Bluetooth or network printer).
4. **Hold**: parks an order without sending it to the kitchen. **Held** resumes it.
5. **Split**: equally, by items, or custom amounts. Each part is a separate payment.
6. **Refund or reprint**: open the closed bill from **Bills**. Refunds need permission and a reason, and can return ingredients to stock.
7. **Close shift**: count the drawer and enter the amount. Expected cash and any difference are shown and reported to managers.

Totals are always calculated by the server. If the screen and the receipt ever disagree, the receipt is correct. Report the difference to your manager.
